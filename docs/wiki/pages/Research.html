Research

Research gives permanent bonuses and can be done at the following locations:

  • Library: Firestone Research Trees, Meteorite Research Trees
  • Exotic Merchant: Exotic Upgrade Trees
  • Alchemist: Experiments Trees
  • Character: Talent Tree, Amulets, Achievements
  • Tavern: Luck Level, Sacred Cards, Ancient Artifacts
  • Guild: Guild Perks, Guild Tree of Life, Personal Tree of Life
  • Battles: Campaign Perks
  • Oracle: Blessings
  • Chaos Rift: League Bonuses, Forbidden Knowledge
  • Scarab's Game: Scarab Level, Lost Inscriptions, Beasts

The following list describes all researches and their locations (* indicates that the research is not present in all trees).
