Guild Shop

Guild Shop

Unlocks at

Level 10
In the Guild Shop you can purchase value bundles with platform currency, which contain guild coins and expedition tokens, which in turn are used to buy upgrades in the Tree of Life. You can also claim free pickaxes or buy them with expedition tokens. Once you have unlocked the Engineer, you can also exchange expedition tokens for jewel chests and other currencies related to war machines here.

Supplies

In the supplies shop, you can claim a free pickaxe every 96 minutes (15 pickaxes per day), up to 30 pickaxes at a time. You can also buy pickaxes with expedition tokens.

Offer Price
1 pickaxe free claim every 96 minutes
5 pickaxes 3,000 expedition tokens
20 pickaxes 10,000 expedition tokens

When you enter the shop for the first time, you get a full claim of 30 pickaxes. There is no limit for the number of pickaxes you can collect in your inventory.
