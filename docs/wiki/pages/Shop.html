Shop

Shop

Unlocks at

Stage 21

The shop can be accessed from the battlefield sidebar, by clicking the + button next to the gem display at the top of the battlefield or from the town screen.

The shop contains offers to buy gems and other items with platform currency and to spend gems for gear chests, scrolls and other game currencies. It also contains the daily login rewards page.

Daily Rewards

Check in every day to get your daily reward. Possible rewards are gems, meteorites, honor, strange dust, expedition tokens and gear chests. If you miss a day, you will not be set back to day 1, but continue from where you left when you check in next time. After 28 days, daily rewards reset and start again with day 1. Even though the design represents a calendar, it has nothing to do with the month in real time. You can keep progress over months and still reach the goal for the 28th reward.
