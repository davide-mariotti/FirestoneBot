Exotic Merchant

Exotic Merchant

Unlocks at

Level 30
You will unlock the Exotic Merchant at level 30. At the Exotic Merchant you can sell scrolls and inventory items in exchange for exotic coins. Also at level 65 you start earning emblems of courage from missions. You can exchange these emblems for various gear chests.

Afterwards, the following four trees repeat cyclically with base level requirements increasing by 10 per tree and both stage and wave skips increasing by 400 per tree:

  • Exotic Upgrade Tree A (trees 22, 26, 30, ...)
  • Exotic Upgrade Tree B (trees 23, 27, 31, ...)
  • Exotic Upgrade Tree C (trees 24, 28, 32, ...)
  • Exotic Upgrade Tree D (trees 25, 29, 33, ...)

The highest exotic upgrade tree is currently 97. You have to completely finish one tree to access the next tree. Every upgrade in each of the trees has 40 levels. The cost of an upgrade increases by 24 exotic coins per upgrade level:

cost = base cost + 24 * current upgrade level

Each upgrade also has a minimum character level requirement which increases by 2 character levels per upgrade level:

level requirement = base level requirement + 2 * current upgrade level

Emblem Market

The emblem market unlocks at character level 65. In the market you can buy gear chests with emblems of courage, jewel chests with emblems of valor and celestial chests with emblems of brotherhood.

Gear chests

Offer Price Remarks
4 common chests 2000 emblems of courage
4 uncommon chests 3000 emblems of courage
5 rare chests 5000 emblems of courage
3 epic chests 5000 emblems of courage

Legendary chests and higher are not for sale in the emblem market.

Jewel chests

Offer Price Remarks
4 wooden chests 2000 emblems of valor
4 iron chests 3000 emblems of valor
5 golden chests 5000 emblems of valor
3 diamond chests 5000 emblems of valor requires 100 campaign stars

Opal chests and higher are not for sale in the emblem market.

Celestial chests

Offer Price Remarks
4 comet chests 2000 emblems of brotherhood
4 lunar chests 3000 emblems of brotherhood
5 solar chests 5000 emblems of brotherhood
3 nebula chests 5000 emblems of brotherhood requires oracle level 98

Cosmic chests and higher are not for sale in the emblem market.
