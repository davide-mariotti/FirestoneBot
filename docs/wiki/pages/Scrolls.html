Scrolls

Scrolls are used to boost the fighting power of your party especially during boss battles. Scrolls can be obtained from running adventure missions and from daily quests. They can also be bought for gems in the Item Shop. There is no limit on the number of scrolls you can collect. Extra scrolls can be sold to the Exotic Merchant for 80 exotic coins each.

List of scrolls

Scroll of Speed
The scroll of speed increases the attack speed of your heroes by 200% for the next 60 seconds (hotkey: 4).

Scroll of Damage
The scroll of damage increases the damage of your heroes by 200% for the next 60 seconds (hotkey: 5).

Scroll of Health
The scroll of health increases the health of your heroes by 200% for the next 60 seconds (hotkey: 6).

When all three scrolls are active, the damage of all your heroes and guardians is further increased according to your Ancient Knowledge talent.
