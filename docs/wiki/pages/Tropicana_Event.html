The tropicana event is a calendar event that starts in June and lasts for two weeks. Previous installments were:

  • Tropicana Event 2022: 21 July to 4 August 2022
  • Tropicana Event 2023: 15 June to 29 June 2023
  • Tropicana Event 2024: 14 June to 28 June 2024
  • Tropicana Event 2025: 13 June to 27 June 2025
  • Tropicana Event 2026: 12 June to 26 June 2026

In this event, Bruce, the shell collector drops shells on the battlefield which then can be exchanged in the souvenir shop for gear chests, jewel chests, certain currencies and unique avatars. In addition, the town features tropical decorations.

Shells

During the event, Bruce, the shell collector drops shells on the battlefield with a rate of 1 shell per 28.8 seconds. The shells are collected automatically which leads to a fixed gain of 3,000 shells per day. Shells are also collected while you are offline and will be credited to you when Bruce visits you the first time after you relogin. Offline gains are capped at 24 hours, though.

If you make a purchase in the shell shop (see below), the drop rate will increase to 2 shells per 28.8 seconds for the remainder of the event.

Souvenir Shop

The souvenir shop replaces the fountain in town and is only present during the tropicana event. After the end of the event, the souvenir shop will vanish again and you will lose your remaining shells. The shop consists of the following three parts.

Shell Exchange
