Seals of Power

Seals of power are used in the Hall of Heroes to upgrade hero gear, jewels and soulstones to legendary and higher rarities. They are specific to a certain equipment item and can only be used to upgrade this item. All seals are unique.

List of Seals

Legendary Seal of Power
Legendary seals drop with a guaranteed 1 in 10 chance from epic chests, legendary chests, diamond chests, opal chests, cosmic chests and galaxy chests. Epic and legendary chests share the same pool, as well as diamond and opal chests. They can be used to upgrade a level 6 epic gear item, jewel or soulstone to legendary rarity.

Mythic Seal of Power
Mythic seals drop with a guaranteed 1 in 15 chance from legendary chests, mythic chests, opal chests, emerald chests and , cosmic chests and galaxy chests. They can be used to upgrade a level 9 legendary gear item, jewel or soulstone to mythic rarity.

Titan Seal of Power
Titan seals drop with a guaranteed 1 in 15 chance from mythic chests,titan chests, emerald chests,platinum chests and galaxy chests and stellar chests. They can be used to upgrade a level 11 mythic gear item, jewel or soulstone to titan rarity.

Angel Seal of Power
Angel seals drop with a guaranteed 1 in 15 chance from titan chests, platinum chests and stellar chests. They can be used to upgrade a level 13 titan gear item, jewel or soulstone to angel rarity.

Duplicates

Duplicate seal drops cannot occur until all seals of the same type are obtained and will then result in a large gain of void crystals, ethereal shards or soul shards instead:

value = 10 * 2tier-1 * 3rarity-1
