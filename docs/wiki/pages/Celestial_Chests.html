Celestial Chests

Celestial chests are obtained by performing rituals in the Oracle. They can also be bought with platform currency in the Oracle's shop. Chests contain soulstones for your heroes and glyphs for your Oracle's blessings.

List of chests

There are various rarities of celestial chests in the game. Higher rarity chests give more glyphs, a higher chance on higher rarity soulstones and a guaranteed chance for seals of power.

Comet Chest
A comet chest will give you 1 random soulstone. You will also get 50 glyphs.

Lunar Chest
A lunar chest will give you 1 uncommon soulstone and 1 random soulstone. You will also get 100 glyphs.

Solar Chest
A solar chest will give you 1 rare soulstone and 2 random soulstones. You will also get 150 glyphs.

Nebula Chest
A nebula chest will give you 1 epic soulstone, 2 rare soulstones and 1 random soulstone. You will also get 300 glyphs. You also have a 1 in 10 chance to get a legendary seal from this chest.

Cosmic Chest
A cosmic chest will give you 3 epic soulstones, 1 rare soulstone and 1 random soulstone. You will also get 600 glyphs. You also have a 1 in 10 chance to get a legendary seal and a 1 in 15 chance to get a mythic seal from this chest.

Galaxy Chest
A galaxy chest will give you 1 legendary soulstone, 3 epic soulstones and 2 random soulstones. You will also get 1200 glyphs. You also have a 1 in 15 chance to get a mythic seal and a 1 in 15 chance to get a titan seal from this chest.

Stellar Chest
A stellar chest will give you 1 mythic soulstone, 3 legendary soulstones and 3 random soulstones. You will also get 2400 glyphs. You also have a 1 in 15 chance to get an titan seal and a 1 in 15 chance to get a angel seal from this chest.

Drop chances

New items

Any soulstone dropped from a celestial chest has a guaranteed chance of being new, that is not a duplicate:

Soulstone rarity Chance of being new
Common 1 in 10
Uncommon 1 in 25
Rare 1 in 25
Epic 1 in 30
Legendary 1 in 10*
Mythic 1 in 10*

 * only applies to direct soulstone draws from galaxy chests and stellar chests

Note that the 1 in x chance to get a new soulstone doesn't mean that you get a new soulstone every x soulstone drops. It only means that you are guaranteed to get a new soulstone in every batch of x soulstones starting with the first soulstone of the type you gained. For a more in-depth explanation of guaranteed rates, see Randomness.
