Town

In the town Irongard you can access most of the game's features through various buildings. Everything inside the buildings are permanent boosts. You do not lose any progress in them when you prestige.

Hall of Heroes (unlocks at Level 1)
Here you find your heroes. You can also upgrade the gear of your heroes with void crystals and seals of power.
Magic Quarter (unlocks at Level 1)
Here you find your guardians. You can train, enlighten and evolve your guardians.
Shop (unlocks at Stage 21)
In the shop you can buy gems and other items with platform currency or spend your gems for chests and other game currencies.
Temple of Eternals (unlocks at Stage 45)
Here you can prestige and earn firestones. You can also convert firestones and gold into fire tokens for research.
Library (unlocks at Stage 45)
Here you can do meteorite research and firestone research.
Guild (unlocks at Level 10)
Takes you to the guild screen.
Pirate Ship (unlocks at Level 10)
Here you can hire mercenaries and claim the pirate's prize.
Tavern (unlocks at Level 15)
Here you can gamble game tokens on random card draws.
Leaderboards (unlocks at Level 10)
The statue leads you to the in-game leaderboards.
Exotic Merchant (unlocks at Level 30)
Here you can sell inventory items and scrolls. You can also buy upgrades with exotic coins. In the emblem market you can also exchange emblems of courage for gear chests and emblems of valor for jewel chests.
Engineer (unlocks at Level 50)
Here you can equip and upgrade your war machines.
Battles (unlocks at Level 50)
Here you can fight battles with your war machines. The building contains the Training Base, the Warfront Campaign and the Arena of Kings.
Alchemist (unlocks at Level 120)
Here you can run experiments and transmute gear chests or jewel chests into higher rarity chests.
Oracle (unlocks at Level 200)
Here you can perform rituals and buy blessings. In the oracle shop you can also buy celestial chests and currencies.
