Guild


Guilds unlock at character level 10 and can be entered by clicking the guild building in Town. In the guild screen, you can access the different guild buildings. If you are currently not in a guild, most of these buildings will be locked. If you want to find a new guild, click the "Find Guild" button on the bottom. For 100 gems you can also create a new guild.

Each guild gives guild perks which depend on the guild level and which can be viewed by clicking the guild icon in the top left corner.

Guild Hall
In the Guild Hall you can see the list of guild members and basic guild information. The guild leader and the guild officers can also manage the guild here.
Expeditions
Guild expeditions take between 5 and 60 minutes and reward you with guild coins and expedition tokens.
Guild Shop
In the Guild Shop you can obtain pickaxes and purchase extra guild coins and expedition tokens with platform currency. Once you have unlocked the Engineer, you can also exchange expedition tokens for jewel chests and other currencies related to war machines here.
Bank
In the Guild Bank you can donate guild coins you purchased in the Guild Shop or obtained in the Arena of Kings to the guild.
Tree of Life
The Tree of Life offers upgrades which can be bought with guild coins or expedition tokens.
Arcane Crystal (unlocks at guild level 5)
Guild members can mine the Crystal with pickaxes to receive arcane crystals, which in turn can be used for the Awakening of heroes.
Awakening
Awakening allows you to levelup your heroes with the help of arcane crystals.
Goblin Merchant
The goblin merchant visits the guild town in his air trading balloon from time to time to present special offers of guild-related currencies.
Chaos Rift
In the Chaos Rift you can battle the dark gods.
Forbidden Knowledge
In Forbidden Knowledge you can do research in the dark gods' domains.
