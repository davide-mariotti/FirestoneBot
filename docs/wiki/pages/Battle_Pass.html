Battle Pass

The battle pass is a series of daily and weekly challenges that give glory when completed. Once you have collected enough glory you can claim chests, currencies or temporary buffs as rewards.

Challenges

There are daily and weekly challenges to complete. Daily challenges reset each day. There are four sets of weekly challenges. Week 1 challenges start on the first day of a calendar month. After 7 days, week 2 challenges are added while the week 1 challenges remain active. Week 3 and week 4 challenges are added similarly. All weekly challenges reset at the end of the calendar month.

Any challenges involving research auto-complete when you have finished all corresponding research trees already.

Rewards

Rewards can be fixed or variable. Variable rewards depend on your stats at the beginning of the month. Any rewards not claimed by the end of the month will be sent to you via game mail.

Golden pass rewards

Golden pass rewards can only be claimed once you have purchased the golden pass. The golden pass is only valid for the current calendar month and can be activated up to one day before the end of the month.

Offer Description Price
Golden pass Unlocks golden pass rewards * or 1500 gems
Next milestone Instantly get glory to reach the next milestone 3 gems per glory

 * price depends on your platform

Golden pass rewards are chests, currencies or temporary buffs and can be claimed when your total glory of the current month reaches the corresponding milestone. Temporary buffs are only active until the end of the current month. Two golden pass buffs of the same type are additive with each other (except for raining gold and all attributes which are multiplicative) and multiplicative with other buffs of the same type.
