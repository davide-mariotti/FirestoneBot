Weekly Rewards

If you subscribe to Holyday Studio's newsletter in the game settings, you will receive a weekly reward code per e-mail, which then can be exchanged for chests or currencies. The reward code is tied to your platform account and can be used on all servers you play on with this account. Reward codes are valid for one week and are sent every Friday.

Rewards

Weekly rewards are pooled random with N being the total number of rewards in the pool (ranging between N=9 and N=30 depending on your character level and your oracle level).
