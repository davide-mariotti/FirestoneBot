The spring event (“Nature's Dance”) is a calendar event that starts in April and lasts for two weeks. Previous installments were:

  • Spring Event 2023: 7 April 2023 to 21 April 2023
  • Spring Event 2024: 12 April 2024 to 26 April 2024
  • Spring Event 2025: 18 April 2025 to 2 May 2025
  • Spring Event 2026: 17 April 2026 to 1 May 2026

In this event, Sandy, the little gardener drops flowers on the battlefield which then can be exchanged in the flower shop for gear chests, jewel chests, celestial chests, certain currencies and unique avatars. In addition, the town features spring decorations.

Flowers

During the event, Sandy, the the little gardener drops flowers on the battlefield with a rate of 1 flower per 28.8 seconds. The flowers are collected automatically which leads to a fixed gain of 3,000 flowers per day. Flowers are also collected while you are offline and will be credited to you when Sandy visits you the first time after you relogin. Offline gains are capped at 24 hours, though.

If you make a purchase in the flower shop (see below), the drop rate will increase to 2 flowers per 28.8 seconds for the remainder of the event.

Flower Exchange

In the flower exchange you can exchange flowers for chests and currencies. The type of gear chest offered depends on your character level and the type of jewel chest offered depends on the number of your campaign stars.
