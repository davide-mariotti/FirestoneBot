Gear Chests

Gear chests are obtained by running various types of missions and from daily quests, weekly quests and daily rewards. They can also be bought with gems in the Gear Chest Shop and with emblems of courage in the Emblem Market. High value chests can also be bought with platform currency in Extreme Value Bundles and Special Offers. Chests contain gear for your heroes and inventory items. Once you have opened 5 chests, an auto chest opening option appears.

List of chests

There are various rarities of chests in the game. Higher rarity chests give more items, a higher chance on higher rarity gear and a guaranteed chance for seals of power.

Common Chest
A common chest will give you 1 random gear item and 1 random inventory item.

Uncommon Chest
An uncommon chest will give you 1 uncommon gear item, 1 random gear item and 2 random inventory items.

Rare Chest
A rare chest will give you 1 rare gear item, 2 random gear items and 3 random inventory items.

Epic Chest
An epic chest will give you 1 epic gear item, 2 rare gear items, 1 random gear item and 4 random inventory items. You also have a 1 in 10 chance to get a legendary seal from this chest.

Legendary Chest
A legendary chest will give you 3 epic gear items, 1 rare gear item, 1 random gear item and 5 random inventory items. You also have a 1 in 10 chance to get a legendary seal and a 1 in 15 chance to get a mythic seal from this chest.

Mythic Chest
A mythic chest will give you 1 legendary gear item, 3 epic gear items, 2 random gear items and 6 random inventory items. You also have a 1 in 15 chance to get a mythic seal and a 1 in 15 chance to get a titan seal from this chest.

Titan Chest
A titan chest will give you 1 mythic gear item, 3 legendary gear items, 3 random gear items and 7 random inventory items. You also have a 1 in 15 chance to get a titan seal and a 1 in 15 chance to get an angel seal from this chest.

Drop chances

Item tier

Any gear item dropped from a gear chest has the following chances to be of a given tier:

Tier Chance
Tier 1 180/285 = 63.2%
Tier 2 75/285 = 26.3%
Tier 3 30/285 = 10.5%

Item rarity

Random gear items dropped from a gear chest have the following chances to be of a given rarity:

New items

Any gear item dropped from a gear chest has a guaranteed chance of being new, that is not a duplicate:

Item rarity Chance of being new
Common 1 in 10
Uncommon 1 in 25
Rare 1 in 25
Epic 1 in 30
Legendary 1 in 10*
Mythic 1 in 10*

 * only applies to direct gear item draws from mythic chests and titan chests

Note that the 1 in x chance to get a new item doesn't mean that you get a new item every x item drops. It only means that you are guaranteed to get a new item in every batch of x items starting with the first item of the type you gained. For a more in-depth explanation of guaranteed rates, see Randomness.

Inventory items

Inventory items are drawn uniformly randomly from the list of 10 possible items with the condition that the same inventory item cannot drop twice from the same gear chest.
