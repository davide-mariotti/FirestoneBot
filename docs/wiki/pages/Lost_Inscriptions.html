Lost Inscriptions

Lost inscriptions are obtained as rewards from the pharaoh's vault. There are four types of lost inscriptions: hero inscriptions, guardian inscriptions, war machine inscriptions and attribute inscriptions. You can only get hero, guardian and war machine inscriptions on heroes, guardians and war machines you own. All inscriptions have a level which is indicated by stars.

Inscription draws are random, but inscriptions which currently have a lower level than your maximum level inscription have a higher chance of being drawn:

  • inscriptions which have 1 level less have a 3x higher chance of being drawn
  • inscriptions which have 2 levels less have a 5x higher chance of being drawn
  • inscriptions which have 3 levels less have a 7x higher chance of being drawn
  • and so on

This mechanism balances out the distribution of levels in your inscriptions over time and also enables new inscriptions (for instance when you gain a new hero) to catch up to your other inscriptions more quickly.

The effect of a inscription is computed as follows:

effect = (1 + base effect)inscription level - 1
