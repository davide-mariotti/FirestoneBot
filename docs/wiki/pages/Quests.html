Quests

Daily and weekly quests can be accessed from the Quests tab in the character menu. All quests update as soon as you reach the corresponding requirement. Any unclaimed quest rewards will be delivered via game mail the following day.

Daily Quests

Daily quests reset every 24 hours at your daily reset time. Some quests have a character level requirement and appear only when the corresponding game feature is unlocked. For some quests, the reward scales with your map rank or your campaign level. There are 9 daily quests in total:

Name Task Reward Requirement
Trainer Train guardians 2 times (20 + 2 × map rank) strange dust
Conqueror Complete 6 map missions (3 + map rank) honor
Collector Open 4 chests 1 random gear chest
Gamer Play 10 times in the tavern 300 beers level 15
Expeditioner Complete 5 guild expeditions 300 expedition tokens level 10
Merchant Sell 10 items at the exotic merchant (100 + 10 × map rank) exotic coins level 30
Liberator Complete 2 liberation missions (98 + 2 × campaign level) blueprints level 50
Miner Hit the arcane crystal 5 times 2 pickaxes level 50
Prophet Complete 2 rituals at the Oracle (20 + 5 × oracle milestone) virtue level 200

The random gear chest has the following probability distribution depending on your character level:

Level 70% chance 20% chance 10% chance
1-69 common chest uncommon chest rare chest
70-139 uncommon chest rare chest epic chest
140-299 rare chest epic chest legendary chest
300-599*: epic chest legendary chest mythic chest
600†- legendary chest mythic chest titan chest

 * also requires more than 5 billion total gear power
 † also requires more than 60 billion total gear power

The chance is not pooled, so there is no guarantee that you receive a chest of the highest available quality after 10 days.
