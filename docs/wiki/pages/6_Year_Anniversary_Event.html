The 6 Year Anniversary Event was a special event to celebrate the sixth birthday of Firestone Idle RPG. The event ran from 1 April 2025 to 15 March 2026 and lasted for two weeks.

The event starts individually for each player. During the event, you earn activity coins simply by being online which then can be exchanged for gear chests, jewel chests, celestial chests, certain currencies and unique avatars or hero skins.

Milestones

During the event, you earn activity coins simply by being logged into the game. At certain online milestones, you automatically get a certain number activity coins. On the first three days of the event, the number of milestones and the amount of activity coins you can earn is smaller than for the rest of the event.

Milestone Online duration Reward Days
1 15 minutes 1400 activity coins day 1-14
2 30 minutes 1250 activity coins day 2-14
3 60 minutes 1200 activity coins day 3-14
4 120 minutes 1150 activity coins day 4-14

If you purchase the event pass in the coin shop (see below), the amount of activity coins you get at online milestones will double for the remainder of the event.

Skins

Here you can exchange activity coins for unique hero skins which can only be obtained during the 6 year anniversary event. The skins just alter the appearence of a hero and don't provide any gameplay bonuses.

Skin Price
Talia, the wild 42,000 activity coins

When you buy a hero skin, you will obtain the corresponding avatar as well.
