Alchemist

Alchemist

Unlocks at

Level 120
You will unlock the Alchemist when you reach character level 120. The Alchemist can run experiments for random upgrades and transmute chests to higher rarities.

Experiments

The upgrades of the Alchemist are called experiments and are randomly decided. The chance to get a specific experiment is:

chance = (remaining experiments of that type) / (total remaining experiments)

You can run 3 experiments at once, each with a different resource:

  • 50 dragon blood
  • 40 strange dust
  • 1250 exotic coins

All experiments can be individually sped up with gem speedup. There are 12 fixed experiments trees:

  • Alchemist Experiments Tree 1
  • Alchemist Experiments Tree 2
  • Alchemist Experiments Tree 3
  • Alchemist Experiments Tree 4
  • Alchemist Experiments Tree 5
  • Alchemist Experiments Tree 6
  • Alchemist Experiments Tree 7
  • Alchemist Experiments Tree 8
  • Alchemist Experiments Tree 9
  • Alchemist Experiments Tree 10
  • Alchemist Experiments Tree 11
  • Alchemist Experiments Tree 12

Afterwards, the following five trees repeat cyclically with experiment durations increasing by 1 hour per tree:

  • Alchemist Experiments Tree A (trees 13, 18, 23, ...)
  • Alchemist Experiments Tree B (trees 14, 19, 24, ...)
  • Alchemist Experiments Tree C (trees 15, 20, 25, ...)
  • Alchemist Experiments Tree D (trees 16, 21, 26, ...)
  • Alchemist Experiments Tree E (trees 17, 22, 27, ...)

The highest alchemist experiments tree is currently 47. You have to fully complete a tree before you can start on the next one.

Transmutation

At character level 130 you unlock transmutation inside the Alchemist.

Gear Chests

Transmutation allows you to convert gear chests to a higher rarity with the help of dragon blood and strange dust.

Ingredient 1 Ingredient 2 Ingredient 3 Result Requirement
1 mythic chest 800 dragon blood 30 strange dust 1 titan chest character level 600 and 60 billion total gear power
1 legendary chest 650 dragon blood 25 strange dust 1 mythic chest character level 300 and 5 billion total gear power
1 epic chest 500 dragon blood 20 strange dust 1 legenday chest
3 rare chests 350 dragon blood 15 strange dust 1 epic chest
3 uncommon chests 200 dragon blood 10 strange dust 1 rare chest
2 common chests 150 dragon blood 5 strange dust 1 uncommon chest

Jewel Chests

If you have at least 70 campaign stars, you can also transmute jewel chests with the help of elixirs of life and strange dust.

Ingredient 1 Ingredient 2 Ingredient 3 Result Requirement
1 emerald chest 800 elixirs of life 30 strange dust 1 platinum chest 319 stars
1 opal chest 650 elixirs of life 25 strange dust 1 emerald chest 190 stars
1 diamond chest 500 elixirs of life 20 strange dust 1 opal chest 145 stars
3 golden chests 350 elixirs of life 15 strange dust 1 diamond chest 100 stars
3 iron chests 200 elixirs of life 10 strange dust 1 golden chest 70 stars
2 wooden chests 150 elixirs of life 5 strange dust 1 iron chest 70 stars

Celestial Chests

If you have at least oracle level 149, you can also transmute celestial chests with the help of star essence and strange dust.

Ingredient 1 Ingredient 2 Ingredient 3 Result Requirement
1 galaxy chest 800 star essence 30 strange dust 1 stellar chest oracle level 279
1 cosmic chest 650 star essence 25 strange dust 1 galaxy chest oracle level 201
1 nebula chest 500 star essence 20 strange dust 1 cosmic chest
3 solar chests 350 star essence 15 strange dust 1 nebula chest
3 lunar chests 200 star essence 10 strange dust 1 solar chest
2 comet chests 150 star essence 5 strange dust 1 lunar chest
