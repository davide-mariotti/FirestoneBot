Mystery Boxes

Mystery boxes can be claimed once per day in the Shop or can be purchased as part of Extreme Value Bundles. During the World Domination mini-event, they can also be obtained from mystery missions. They appear in your inventory next to gear chests and jewel chests.

Rewards

When you open a mystery box, a reward is randomly drawn from your current pool of rewards. The chance for each possible reward is a guaranteed rate where N is the total number of rewards in the reward pool (ranging between N=44 and N=85 depending on your character level). The number of drawn items is uniformly random within the range for that reward.
