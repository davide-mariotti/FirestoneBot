Achievements

There are 34 achievements in the game, each with a specific type of requirement, a certain number of levels and a specific type of bonus earned from completing a level. When you complete a level of an achievement you will get an in-game popup message that says you have completed it along with the added bonus. In addition to these in-game achievements, some platforms also have platform achievemets.

Bonus

There are five types of bonuses. The bonus amount earned can change at various achievement levels. All bonuses of the same type are additive per level, even across different achievements. The gold earnings and all attributes bonuses are multiplicative with the other upgrades of the same type.

Bonus Type Number of Achievements
All main attributes 15
Raining gold 16
Skip stage 1
Skip wave 1
Guarding training xp 1
