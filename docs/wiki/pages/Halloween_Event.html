The halloween event ("Trick or Treat") is a calendar event that starts in October and lasts for two weeks. Previous installments were:

  • Halloween Event 2022: 24 October to 7 November 2022
  • Halloween Event 2023: 20 October to 3 November 2023
  • Halloween Event 2024: 25 October to 8 November 2024
  • Halloween Event 2025: 24 October to 7 November 2025

In this event, Maxwell, the pumpkin head drops pumpkins on the battlefield which then can be exchanged in the witch's hut for gear chests, jewel chests, certain currencies and unique avatars. In addition, the town features halloween decorations.

Pumpkins

During the event, Maxwell, the pumpkin head drops pumpkins on the battlefield with a rate of 1 pumpkin per 28.8 seconds. The pumpkins are collected automatically which leads to a fixed gain of 3,000 pumpkins per day. Pumpkins are also collected while you are offline and will be credited to you when Maxwell visits you the first time after you relogin. Offline gains are capped at 24 hours, though.

If you make a purchase in the pumpkin shop (see below), the drop rate will increase to 2 pumpkins per 28.8 seconds for the remainder of the event.

Pumpkin Exchange

In the pumpkin exchange you can exchange pumpkins for chests and currencies. The type of gear chest offered depends on your character level and the type of jewel chest offered depends on the number of your campaign stars.
