Inventory Items

Inventory items are used to increase your gold and meteorite gain or to boost the fighting power of your party especially during boss battles. Inventory items can be found in gear chests or can be gained from daily quests. They are collected in your bag which can be accessed from the battle screen menu. There is no limit on the number of inventory items you can collect. Extra inventory items can be sold to the Exotic Merchant for exotic coins.

List of inventory items

Midas' Touch
Midas' Touch increases your gold earnings by 200% for 2 minutes.

Pouch of Gold
Pouch of gold instantly awards you the gold and meteorites you would earn in 5 minutes.

Bucket of Gold
Bucket of gold instantly awards you the gold and meteorites you would earn in 15 minutes.

Crate of Gold
Crate of gold instantly awards you the gold and meteorites you would earn in 30 minutes.

Pile of Gold
Pile of gold instantly awards you the gold and meteorites you would earn in 60 minutes.

Drums of War
Drums of War increases the damage of your heroes by 100% for 2 minutes. Unlocks at stage 20.

Dragon Armor
Dragon Armor increases the armor of your heroes by 100% for 2 minutes. Unlocks at stage 35.

Guardian's Rune
Guardian's Rune increases the damage of your guardian by 300% for 2 minutes. Unlocks at stage 40.

Totem of Agony
Totem of Agony decreases the health of your enemies by 50% for 2 minutes. Unlocks at stage 50.

Totem of Annihilation
Totem of Annihilation shoots 5 lightning bolts per second at your enemies for 2 minutes. Lightning bolt damage depends on the damage of your heroes and your guardian. Unlocks at stage 70.

Instant Gold Item Boost

Items that gives instant amount of gold can give more gold by increasing the damage and attack speed of your party. You might have to be at a higher stage to take full advantage of the gold gain boost from the increased damage and attack speed. Even though midas' touch increases the gold gain from the enemy deaths and totem of agony decreases their health so they die quicker, those items don't have an effect on the gold gains from the instant gold items, like drums of war has. Instant gold item are also boosted by scrolls (scroll of speed and scroll of damage directly and along with scroll of health indirectly as ancient knowledge), twilight of the gods and some hero abilities like Boris' Anger, Muriel's Rally and Valerius' Blessing of the Defender.
