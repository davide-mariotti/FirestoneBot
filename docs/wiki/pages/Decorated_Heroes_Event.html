Description

Type

Calendar event

Date

January, March, May, July, September, November

Duration

2 weeks

Unlocks at

Character level 50
The decorated heroes event is a calendar event that takes place in the odd months of the year. Previous installments of this event were

  • 12 July to 26 July 2024
  • 13 September to 27 September 2024
  • 15 November to 29 November 2024
  • 17 January to 31 January 2025
  • 14 March to 28 March 2025
  • 16 May to 30 May 2025
  • 11 July to 25 July 2025
  • 15 September to 29 September 2025
  • 14 November to 28 November 2025
  • 16 January to 30 January 2026
  • 13 March to 27 March 2026
  • 15 May to 29 May 2026
  • 17 July to 31 July 2026

During the event, you can earn stars of recognition by completing daily challenges. The stars you earned can then be exchanged for gear chests, jewel chests, celestial chests, certain currencies or unique hero skins. If you earn enough stars during the event, you will be awarded a medal of recognition which permanently boosts your fate attribute.

Challenges

You can earn stars of recognition by completing the following eight challenges. Each challenge has three tiers with increasing total requirements and different rewards for each tier. All challenges reset daily.

Challenges (Tier 1/2/3) Rewards (Tier 1/2/3) Requirement
Complete 1/2/3 cycles of scout missions 70/30/50 stars of recognition
Enlighten guardians 1/2/3 times 40/20/30 stars of recognition
Complete 4/6/10 firestone researches 55/25/45 stars of recognition
Conduct 3/6/9 alchemy experiments 60/40/45 stars of recognition character level 120
Hit the arcane crystal 5/10/15 times 35/25/30 stars of recognition
Play 4/8/12 times in the tavern 25/20/35 stars of recognition
Complete 5/10/15 guild expeditions 85/30/45 stars of recognition
Stay online for 15/30/60 minutes 75/35/50 stars of recognition

If you purchase the event pass in the shop (see below), the stars of recognition you get from completing challenges will double for the remainder of the event.

Medals

If you earn enough stars of recognition during the event, you will be awarded a medal of recognition. The medal permanently increases your fate attribute multiplicatively. You will only get the highest medal you reached.

Reward Requirement Fate Bonus
Bronze medal of recognition 8,000 stars of recognition 5%
Silver medal of recognition 10,000 stars of recognition 10%
Gold medal of recognition 13,000 stars of recognition 25%

The design of the medal changes with each iteration of the event.
