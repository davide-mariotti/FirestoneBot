Guild Perks

Guild perks are bonuses which scale with the guild level and apply to all members of the guild. You can view your current guild perks by clicking the guild banner in the top left corner of the guild screen.

List of Guild Perks

There are currently 8 different guild perks. All upgrades are additive per level.
