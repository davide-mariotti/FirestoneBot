Character

The character displayed in the top left corner is you. You manage the heroes and their gear and do missions, research and experiments. When you reach certain milestones in the game you level or rank up.

Character Menu

You can access the character menu by clicking on the character icon on the top-left of the battlefield. The character menu leads to:

  • Character information
  • Talents
  • Achievements
  • Statistics
  • Quests

Character Information

Your character information includes your

  • character name, platform and flag
  • avatar
  • character level and current xp
  • character description
  • guild and member rank
  • map rank and number of map missions won
  • maximum stage
  • number of achievements
  • list of heroes and their total power
  • owned amulets

All this information (except current xp, map missions and amulets) is also shown to other players on character inspect. You can change your character name or your flag for 100 gems (the first flag change is free). Changing your description or your avatar are always free.
