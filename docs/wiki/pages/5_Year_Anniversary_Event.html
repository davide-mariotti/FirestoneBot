Description

Type

Special event

Date

1 April 2024 - 15 March 2025

Unlocks at

Character level 10

The 5 Year Anniversary Event is a special event to celebrate the fifth birthday of Firestone Idle RPG. The event runs from 1 April 2024 to 15 March 2025 and lasts for two weeks. The event starts individually for each player. During the event, you earn activity coins simply by being online which then can be exchanged for gear chests, jewel chests, celestial chests, certain currencies and unique avatars or hero skins.

Milestones

During the event, you earn activity coins simply by being logged into the game. At certain online milestones, you automatically get a certain number activity coins. On the first three days of the event, the number of milestones and the amount of activity coins you can earn is smaller than for the rest of the event. The required online durations differ between desktop and mobile platforms (Google Play and App Store/IOS).

Milestone Online duration Reward Days
Desktop Mobile
1 1 hour 15 minutes 1400 activity coins day 1-14
2 2 hours 30 minutes 1250 activity coins day 2-14
3 4 hours 60 minutes 1200 activity coins day 3-14
4 8 hours 120 minutes 1150 activity coins day 4-14

If you purchase the event pass in the coin shop (see below), the amount of activity coins you get at online milestones will double for the remainder of the event.

Skins

Here you can exchange activity coins for unique hero skins which can only be obtained during the 5 year anniversary event. The skins just alter the appearence of a hero and don't provide any gameplay bonuses.
