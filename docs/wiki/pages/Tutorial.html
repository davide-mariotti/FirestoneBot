Tutorial

The tutorial helps new players learn the basics of the game. It consists of four chapters containing various quests. Upon completion of each chapter, the player is rewarded with gear chests and currencies.

Chapters

Chapter 1: Save Solaine

Quests:

  • Kill 2 enemies with the fairy.
  • Kill one boss.
  • Reach stage 3.
  • Upgrade the fairy 4 times.
  • Unlock hero Burt - reach stage 4.
  • Kill 35 enemies.
  • Unlock hero Solaine - reach stage 8.

Completion rewards: 1 common chest and 1 uncommon chest

Chapter 2: Gather Allies

Quests:

  • Train the fairy - reach stage 9.
  • Reach stage 12.
  • Start a mission οn the map.
  • Reach stage 15.
  • Get 12 special upgrades.
  • Claim your daily reward.
  • Use 3 Scrolls of any type.
  • Use 3 Inventory items of any type.
  • Get 12 achievements.
  • Open 2 Chests.
  • Reach character level 3.
  • Unlock hero Boris - reach stage 30.

Completion rewards: 1 uncommon chest and 40 strange dust

Chapter 3: Prestige

Quests:

  • Enlighten guardians 2 times.
  • Reach character level 5.
  • Use 5 talent points.
  • Kill 800 enemies.
  • [Unless playing as guest] Join or create a fellowship.
  • Do 1000 attacks with the fairy.
  • Claim 1 mission reward from the map.
  • Unlock the library - reach stage 45.
  • Complete a meteorite research.
  • Complete Prestige once.
  • Join or create a fellowship.

Completion rewards: 1 rare chest and 10 gems

Chapter 4: Fresh start

Quests:

  • Upgrade your heroes 200 times.
  • Upgrade a hero 100 times.
  • Complete 3 meteorite researches.
  • Kill 80 enemies with hero "Solaine" as leader.
  • Complete 3 map missions.
  • Collect 350 meteorites.
  • Complete 8 meteorite researches.
  • Reach character level 15 and unlock the tavern.

Completion rewards: 1 rare chest and 10 game tokens
