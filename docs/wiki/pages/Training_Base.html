Training Base

Training Base

Unlocks at

Level 50
In the Training Base, you can assemble up to 3 squads from your pool of war machines and heroes and let them fight against each other. After each fight you will receive the battle statistics of all machines participating in the fight to analyze. The Training Base unlocks at character level 50 and is accessible from the Battles building.
