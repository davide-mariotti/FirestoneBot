Chaos Rift

Chaos Rift

Unlocks at

Level 100

The Chaos Rift is a server-wide activity in which all players on the same game server battle one of the dark gods (Ledra, Yamanoth and Kramatak) in a monthly succession. It can be accessed from the Guild screen.

Dark trial

All players on the same server can attack the current dark god with the help of their guardians. Each attack requires 1 moon stone or 1 eclipse stone. All your guardians will attack simultaneously. Thereby, each guardian's damage is calculated from its chaos rift attributes. A guardian's basic damage is determined by its holy damage attribute. Critical hits can occur according to its precision hit chance attribute and the guardian's damage is then increased by its precision hit damage attribute. Double hits occur with a chance according to the guardian's double strike attribute. Critical and double hits can also stack, so there are the following four possible cases:

basic hit = holy damage

critical hit = (1 + precision hit damage) × holy damage

double hit = 2 × holy damage

double critical hit = 2 × (1 + precision hit damage) × holy damage

The health of a dark god starts at 5 million hit points and increases multiplicatively by 5% per level:

health = 5,000,000 × (1 + 5%)god level-1

When a dark god is killed, its level will automatically increase by one.

Rewards

When a dark god is killed, all players who have hit the dark god at least once will receive rewards. There are two types of rewards: the damage rewards and the rank reward.

Damage rewards

The damage rewards are proportional to the damage the player dealt to the dark god:

Reward Amount
Dark runes 30% of the player's damage
Orbs of light 25% of the player's damage

Dark runes are used to buy tomes of power of the current dark god in the supplies shop. Orbs of light are used to upgrade the holy damage of your guardians. At the end of the month, any remaining dark runes will be automatically converted into tomes of power of the current dark god and reset. All holy damage upgrades and any remaining orbs of light will be reset at the end of the month too.

Rank reward

The top 5 players on the damage leaderboard will additionally get the following rewards when the dark god is killed:

Rank Reward
1 10 tomes of power (current god)
2 7 tomes of power (current god)
3 5 tomes of power (current god)
4 3 tomes of power (current god)
5 2 tomes of power (current god)

Server bonus

When a dark god is killed, the whole server gets a 100% all attributes bonus called "Twilight of the gods" for 60 minutes (stacking in time).

Supplies

In the supplies shop, you can buy tomes of power of the current god with dark runes. You can also buy eclipse stones with expedition tokens.

Offer Cost
1 tome of power (current god) 3,500* dark runes
4 eclipse stones 2,500 expedition tokens
10 eclipse stones 5,000 expedition tokens

* The cost multiplicatively increases by 4.5% after each purchase. This cost increase resets at the end of each month.
