Unlock Requirements

The unlock requirements for each column are:

  • Column 2 requires 6 levels in the previous research
  • Column 3 requires 7 levels in the previous researches
  • Column 4 requires 8 levels in the previous researches
  • Column 5 requires 5 levels in the previous research
  • Column 6 requires 7 levels in the previous researches
  • Column 7 requires 10 levels in the previous researches
  • Column 8 requires 10 levels in the previous researches

Research Duration

The research duration starts at 16 hours 40 minutes for level 1 and increases gradually over the next 10 levels up to 26 hours.
