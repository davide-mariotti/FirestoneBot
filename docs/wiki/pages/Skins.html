Skins

Skins are obtained when you reach a certain milestone or when you purchase them in certain events. Skins just alter the appearence of a hero and don't provide any gameplay bonuses. When you obtain a skin you will also get the corresponding avatar.

Milestone skins

These skins are obtained when you reach a certain milestone:
