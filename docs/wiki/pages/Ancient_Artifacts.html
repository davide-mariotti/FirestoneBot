Ancient Artifacts

Ancient artifacts are unique items which give large permanent bonuses. They are obtained in the Tavern either by drawing an artifact card or by crafting one with 5 Primordial Elements. You get the artifacts in a random order. Each artifact has a level and a rarity.

Enchanting

Artifacts can have one of eight rarities (Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Titan, Angel, Celestial, Immortal and Primordial). The rarity of an artifact can be increased by enchanting it with golden keys. The enchantment cost starts at 20 keys and increases by 5 keys for the first two rarity upgrades, and then increases by 10 keys for the final rarity upgrade. Each rarity generally doubles the effect of the artifact attributes. But it increases by ×4 for health and armor attributes, by 0.5% for resource regenerations, the alchemy perk and the librarian perk, by 0.2% for the explorer perk and by 5% for wWar machine attributes.

Upgrading

The level of an artifact can be increased with twilight hourglasses until a level cap is reached which depends on the rarity of the artifact. The upgrade cost starts at 5 twilight hourglasses per level for epic artifacts and increases by 1 hourglass for each higher rarity up to a cap of 10 hourglasses. Each level upgrade increases all attributes of the artifact, except for regenerations, alchemy/librarian/explorer and war machine attributes, multiplicatively by 6% (12.36% for health and armor). The maximum level of an artifact is 100.
