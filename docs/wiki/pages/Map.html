Map

The map is the place where you run missions. From missions you obtain most of the resources you need to progress further: strange dust for your guardians, scrolls for temporarily buffing your heroes or to sell them for exotic coins at the Exotic Merchant and gear chests to obtain gear, void crystals and inventory items. Character level 65 will unlock emblems of courage as an additional reward from completing missions and higher levels will unlock additional mission types with unique mission rewards.

Missions

Each mission type has a baseline duration, needs a specific amount of squads and gives specific rewards. All mission durations vary in a 20% interval from the baseline duration. Mission durations can be decreased with expeditioner upgrades. All missions can be sped up with gem speedup.

Map refresh

The map refreshes every 6 hours. If you are offline after a refresh should occure the refresh will happens once you are online again. If you have finished all missions before the next map reset, a map refresh button appears in the bottom-left corner of the map. The map reset button allows you to immediately refresh the map for the gem price according to gem speedup. Any unstarted missions will dissapear after a map refresh.

Mission types

Scout Mission
The baseline duration is 30 minutes to complete this mission. It requires 1 squad. It will reward you with 10 strange dust, 1 honor and 5 emblems.

Adventure Mission
The baseline duration is 1 hour to complete this mission. It requires 1 squad. It will reward you with 2 random scrolls, 2 honor and 10 emblems.

War Mission
The baseline duration is 2 hours to complete this mission. It requires 1 squad. It will reward you with a random gear chest, 4 honor and 20 emblems. There is a 7 in 10 chance the chest will be common. 2 in 10 chance for uncommon. 1 in 10 chance for rare.

Monster Mission
Unlocks at character level 70. A monster mission has a guaranteed 1 in 3 chance of appearing. The baseline duration is 7 hours to complete this mission. It requires 2 squads. It will reward you with an epic chest, 28 honor and 140 emblems.

Dragon Mission
Unlocks at character level 120. The baseline duration 4 hours to complete this mission. It requires 2 squads. It will reward you with 50 dragon blood, 16 honor and 80 emblems.

Naval Mission
Unlocks at character level 140. A naval mission has a guaranteed 1 in 4 chance of appearing. The baseline duration is 7 hours to complete this mission. It requires 2 squads. It will reward you with a legendary chest, 28 honor and 140 emblems.

Titan Mission
Unlocks at character level 300 and 5 billion gear power. A titan mission has a guaranteed 1 in 4 chance of appearing. The baseline duration is 7 hours to complete this mission. It requires 2 squads. It will reward you with a mythic chest, 28 honor and 140 emblems.

Shadow Mission
Unlocks at character level 600 and 60 billion gear power. A shadow mission has a guaranteed 1 in 4 chance of appearing. The baseline duration is 7 hours to complete this mission. It requires 2 squads. It will reward you with a titan chest, 28 honor and 140 emblems.

Mystery Mission
Mystery missions only appear during the World Domination mini-event. The baseline duration is 5 minutes to complete this mission. It requires 1 squad. They will reward you with 1 mystery box (4 mystery boxes if you purchased one of the event packs), 1 honor and 5 emblems.

Quality missions

Silver Mission
Silver missions appear randomly on the map once you reach rank 10. They are upgraded regular missions (including Monster, Dragon, and Naval missions) and indicated by silver stars over the mission icon. Silver missions give double rewards, but also require twice the number of squads. Each rank above 9 until 31 increases the number of silver missions on the map by one.

Honor and Emblems Rewards

The amount of honor and emblems you earn from completing missions is determined by the amount of squads required and baseline mission duration:

honor = squads required × baseline duration / 30 minutes.

emblems = 5 × squads required × baseline duration / 30 minutes.

Rank

Your map rank is determined by the total honor you have gained. The ranks goes from 1 to 40. Each rank increases the number of squads available by 1 and the honor effect. Rank 2-9 increases the number of scout, adventure and war missions with a total of 9 scout, 8 adventure and 9 war missions. From rank 10 the amount of silver missions are increased with a total of 23 silver missions. Additional squads can be obtained with the Amulet of Conquest and temporarily from purchasing the Golden Pass.

Honor Effect

The honor effect along with the total amount of honor gives a multiplier to all main attributes of all heroes and guardians:

all attributes multiplier = (1 + total honor × honor effect)
