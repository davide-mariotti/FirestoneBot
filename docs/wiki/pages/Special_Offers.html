Special Offers

There are four types of special offers: starter offers, battlefield offers, guild offers and oracle offers. All special offers can only be purchased with platform currency.

Merchant ship

The merchant ship appears in town next to the pirate ship after seven days of playtime (or when you reach the corresponding level requirement) and stays there for one week. It will periodically return after a week of absence. Merchant ship offers can only be purchased with platform currency. They contain gems, gear chests, void crystals, honor, exotic coins and meteorites. Only 6 packs in total from any type can be purchased. Which packs are offered depends on your character level:

Goblin balloon

Guild offers are brought by the goblin merchant in his air trading balloon which appears in the middle of the guild town. They also last 1 week and when they expire, it will take another week before they return again. Guild offers can only be purchased with platform currency. Until you unlock the Warfront campaign, guild offers contain gems, rare chests, strange dust, void crystals, honor and meteorites. Afterwards they contain guild coins, expedition tokens, jewel chests, ethereal shards, blueprints and pickaxes. Only 6 packs in total from any type can be purchased. Which packs are offered depends on your character level and your number of campaign stars:
