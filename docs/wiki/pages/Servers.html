Servers

The game supports multiple game servers you can play on. You can switch your current server any time by clicking the "switch server" button in the settings menu. On different servers you will have different characters with different progress. Switching servers will not wipe your progress on your previous server.

Server events

Opening event

To celebrate the opening of a new server, a special event takes place that lasts for 30 days. Players who are in the top 50 in the max stage global leaderboard (on the new server only) at the end of the event will receive the following rewards:

Rank Gems Expedition tokens Chests Artifact
1 15,000 150,000 30 1 (random)
2 12,000 120,000 20 -
3 10,000 100,000 15 -
4–10 7,000 70,000 10 -
11–25 5,000 50,000 5 -
26–50 3,500 35,000 3 -

The type of chests received depends on the character level of the player at the end of the event:

Level Chest type
1–64 Rare Chest
65–119 Epic Chest
120– Legendary Chest

The event rewards will be distributed via in-game mail after the end of the event.

Warfront event

In addition to the opening event, a warfront campaign event will take place that lasts for 7 weeks. Players who are in the top 50 of the campaign stars server leaderboard at the end of the event will get the following rewards:

Rank Expedition tokens Blueprints Pickaxes Jewel chests
1 150,000 30,000 300 30
2 120,000 24,000 240 20
3 100,000 20,000 200 15
4–10 70,000 14,000 140 10
11–25 50,000 10,000 100 5
26–50 35,000 7,000 70 3

The type of jewel chests received depends on the number of campaign stars of the player at the end of the event:

Campaign stars Chest type
1–99 Golden chests
100–144 Diamond chests
145– Opal Chests

If two or more players have the same number of campaign stars at the end of the event, they all will get the rewards of the highest ranked player. The event rewards will be distributed via in-game mail after the end of the event.
