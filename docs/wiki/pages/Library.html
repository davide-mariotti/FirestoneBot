At the Library you can do various researches.

Meteorite Research

Here you use meteorites to do research. Research here has no timer, they are instantly completed. You can find all the bonuses the meteorite tree gives you on the left side of the screen. They are all multipliers. There are 21 fixed trees in meteorite research:

  • Meteorite Research Tree A (trees 22, 25, 28, ...)
  • Meteorite Research Tree B (trees 23, 26, 29, ...)
  • Meteorite Research Tree C (trees 24, 27, 30, ...)

The highest meteorite research tree is currently 74.

Firestone Research

Firestone research has a timer and you can only run two researches simultaneously after you unlock the second research slot. There are 21 fixed trees in firestone research:

Afterwards, the following three trees repeat cyclically with research duration increasing by 1 hour per tree:

  • Firestone Research Tree A (trees 22, 25, 28, ...)
  • Firestone Research Tree B (trees 23, 26, 29, ...)
  • Firestone Research Tree C (trees 24, 27, 30, ...)

The highest firestone research tree is currently 51.

All firestone researches can be sped up with gem speedup.
