The space event (“Astral Alignment”) is a calendar event that starts in August and lasts for two weeks. Previous installments were:

  • Space Event 2023: 18 August 2023 to 1 September 2023
  • Space Event 2024: 16 August 2024 to 30 August 2024
  • Space Event 2025: 15 August 2025 to 29 August 2025
  • Space Event 2026: 14 August 2026 to 28 August 2026

In this event, Rocky, the astral explorer drops energy capsules on the battlefield which then can be exchanged in the observatory for gear chests, jewel chests, celestial chests, certain currencies and unique avatars. In addition, the town features space decorations.

Energy Capsules

During the event, Rocky, the astral explorer drops energy capsules on the battlefield with a rate of 1 capsule per 28.8 seconds. The capsules are collected automatically which leads to a fixed gain of 3,000 capsules per day. Capsules are also collected while you are offline and will be credited to you when Rocky visits you the first time after you relogin. Offline gains are capped at 24 hours, though.

If you make a purchase in the capsule shop (see below), the drop rate will increase to 2 capsules per 28.8 seconds for the remainder of the event.

Observatory

The observatory replaces the fountain in town and is only present during the space event. After the end of the event, the observatory will vanish again and you will lose your remaining capsules. The observatory consists of the following three parts.

The type of gear chest offered depends on your character level and the type of jewel chest offered depends on the number of your campaign stars.
