Gold Upgrades

Gold upgrades can be bought while fighting on the battlefield. They reset with every prestige and have to be bought anew. There are two types of gold upgrades:

  • Guardian and hero upgrades, strengthening your guardian or a specific hero in your party.
  • Special upgrades, which buff Guardian Damage, Hero Damage, Hero Health, Hero Armor and Gold Earnings

Guardian and hero upgrades

Guardian and hero gold upgrades boost the damage of your guardian or all attributes (damage, health and armor) of a hero in your party. The cost of these upgrades starts with 100 gold for the first upgrade and increases by 12% per upgrade level:

cost = 100 * 1.12level-2 gold

This cost can be reduced with the Level up Cost research.

The effect of these upgrades increases linearly with the upgrade level with an additional x2 boost every 25 upgrade levels and an additional x1.5 boost every 100 upgrade levels (yielding a total of x3 every 100 levels):

effect = level * 2⌊level / 25⌋ * 1.5⌊level / 100⌋

Since the cost of an upgrade increases by a factor of 1.12 per level, the cost of successive milestones (25 level increments) increases by a factor of 1.1225 ≈ 17.
