Game Chat

The game chat function unlocks at character level 3 and can be accessed by pressing the chat icon in the lower left side of the battlefield. On the same server, players from all game platforms share the same game chat.

Chatrooms

On each server, there are 16 global chatrooms, each one dedicated to one of the game's languages. The default room is English. You can change your current chatroom by accessing the "Global Rooms" menu on top of the chat window. The number of currently playing players on all servers together is displayed in the top right corner of the chat window.

Once you have joined a guild, the guild chatroom tab can be accessed. Here you can exclusively talk to the players currently in your guild. Once you have joined a fellowship, the fellowship chatroom can be accessed, where you can exclusively talk to the players in your fellowship.

You can also send private messages to another player after you added the player to your friend list by clicking on the player's name and then on the friend icon. Your friend list is limited to 50 players.

When there are new messages in the global, guild, fellowship or private chatrooms, a red dot is shown on top of the chatroom select button.

Messaging

The maximum number of characters in a single message is 250 including spaces and emoticons. In the English room, only latin letters can be used, while in other language rooms special characters may be allowed. You can also chose from 15 emoticons, but the maximum number of emoticons you can use in single message is 5.

Above the text message, the player's flag, the player's name and the time the message was sent are displayed. Next to the message, also the player's avatar and the player's character level is shown. Chat moderators are marked with Mod: and game developers are marked with Dev: before their name. Moderators can globally mute a player who is breaking the chat rules for a limited amount of time.

You can individually mute a player by clicking on the player name and then pressing the "Mute" button in the player inspect window. If the player is on your friend list, you have to unfriend the player first. Messages of muted players are not shown to you. You can mute up to 50 players.

Settings

The chat settings can be accessed by clicking the gear icon in the top left corner of the chat window.

  • Chat compact: increase and decrease the window size of the chat for better gameplay depending on the resolution or the platform you play in.
  • Last Message Preview: enables you to see the last posted message in the bottom left corner of battlefield without having the chat window open.
  • Letters Size: adjust the text size from 24 px up to 34 px.
  • Mute list: here you can see every player you have muted and the option to remove (unmute) the player.
  • Chat rules: here you can read the chat rules
