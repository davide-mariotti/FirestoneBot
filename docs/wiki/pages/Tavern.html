Tavern Market

In the tavern market, you can exchange beer or gems for game tokens.

Offer Price
5 game tokens 1500 beers
20 game tokens 1000 gems
5 game tokens 300 gems

Since beers drop with a rate of 1 beer per 28.8 seconds (3000 beers per day), you can obtain 10 game tokens per day from beers this way. The daily beer gain can be increased with the Amulet of Luck and the Cheers guild perk.

Card Draws

Card draws require game tokens and yield various currencies, scrolls, inventory items, gear chests, sacred cards, primordial elements and ancient artifacts as rewards. Each draw also increases your luck depending on the type and amount of rewards drawn:

luck gain = number of items * reward luck multiplier

Once you have gathered 800 luck, an auto play button appears in the tavern.
