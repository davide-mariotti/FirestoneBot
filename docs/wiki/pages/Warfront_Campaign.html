Warfront Campaign

Warfront Campaign

Unlocks at

Level 50
In the Warfront Campaign, you can fight battles with your war machines against enemy war machines. In these battles, you use the battle formation and crew you have selected in the Engineer. Battle rewards include jewel chests, emblems of valor and blueprints. The campaign unlocks at character level 50 and is accessible from the Battles building.

Campaign

The warfront campaign consists of 90 missions and has 5 difficulties (easy, normal, hard, insane and nightmare). The mission locations follow a path on the map through the following areas:

Enemies

The goal of each mission is to beat 5 enemy war machines within 20 battle rounds. Enemy war wachines all have identical damage, health and armor and no special abilities. Enemy attributes scale with the campaign difficulty and the mission number. The base enemy attributes for the first mission on easy difficulty are:

Attribute Damage Health Armor
Base value 260 1,560 30

At higher difficulties, all enemy attributes are multiplied with the following difficulty multipliers:

Difficulty Easy Normal Hard Insane Nightmare
Multiplier 1 360 2,478,600 5.8 × 1012 2.92 × 1018

For each subsequent mission, all enemy attributes increase by 20% (multiplicatively) per mission. In addition, every 10 missions (at mission 11, 21, 31, etc.), all enemy attributes increase by another factor of 3:

enemy attribute = base attribute * difficulty multiplier * 1.2(mission number - 1) * 3⌊(mission number - 1)/10⌋

The enemy team composition cycles through 20 different variations of the following five different types of enemies:

Power requirements

Each mission also has a power requirement for your team, which depends on the power rating of the enemy war machines as follows:

  • the first 10 missions on easy difficulty require 30% of the enemy power
  • missions 11–30 on easy difficulty require 50% of the enemy power
  • all other missions require 80% of the enemy power

The required power is rounded down to the next multiple of 100. For the power rating calculation, an attribute multiplier of 2 instead of 3 for every 10 missions is used.

Loot claim

Once you have beaten your first campaign mission you can claim loot on the campaign map every 6 hours. The loot depends on your campaign level and consists of emblems of valor and blueprints. The loot for campaign level 1 is 400 emblems and 100 blueprints. For each subsequent campaign level, the loot increases by 8 emblems and 2 blueprints.

Daily missions

Daily missions are similar to campaign missions, but can be repeated every day. Each daily mission requires a certain number of campaign stars to start. Enemy war machines in daily missions have 70% of the stats of the enemy war machines of your highest beaten mission and are therefore easy to beat. There are two types of daily missions: liberation missions and dungeon missions.
