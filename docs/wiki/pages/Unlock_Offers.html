Unlock Offers

Unlock offers are special offers that appear when a player unlocks a new game feature. There are four types of unlock offers: hero offers, guardian offers, war machine offers and building offers. All unlock offers can only be purchased with platform currency.

Hero unlock offers

Hero unlock offers appear in the Hall of Heroes whenever you unlock a new hero, mercenary or dark god and last for 7 days. The type of gear chest offered depends on your character level.

Hero Unlock Offer
550 gems
level 1-64:            5 rare chests
level 65-129:       5 epic chests
level 130-299:    5 legendary chests
level 300-599*: 5 mythic chests
level 600**-:      5 titan chests
3000 hero xp
Price: ***

 * also requires more than 5 billion total gear power
 ** also requires more than 60 billion total gear power
 *** price depends on your platform
