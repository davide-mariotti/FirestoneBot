Oracle's Gifts

Oracle's gifts can be claimed once per day in the Oracle Shop or can be obtained from conducting the Oracle's Concentration ritual.

Rewards

When you open an oracle's gift, a reward is randomly drawn from your current pool of rewards. The chance for each possible reward is a guaranteed rate where N=101 is the total number of rewards in the reward pool.
